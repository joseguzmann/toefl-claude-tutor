# Official materials

**Official tests measure. Generated exercises train.** Use the free official material for the
diagnostic, checkpoints and the final mock exam, and let the tutor generate the daily practice.

Download everything into `materials/` (it's in `.gitignore`: ETS material is copyrighted and
shouldn't be redistributed) and tell the tutor what you added.

## Free, from ETS

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| **7 full practice tests** in the 2026 format (PDF, with answer keys): 2 full-length tests for students + 5 teacher-resources tests | [ETS · Preparing students](https://www.ets.org/toefl/teachers-advisors-agents/ibt/teaching/preparing-students.html) |
| The audio for those tests | same page, downloaded separately |
| **TOEFL iBT Test Overview** (PDF): specs, structure and the official rubrics | [ETS · TOEFL iBT](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about.html) |

⚠️ **The PDFs print the Listening scripts and the Speaking sentences**, and each section's answer
key comes right after it. Let the tutor prepare a checkpoint from a PDF and tell you which pages
not to open.

## Paid, worth it

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **The Official Guide to the TOEFL iBT Test** (2026 edition, ETS) | rubrics, task taxonomies, model answers commented by raters, and a full practice test. The top source of authority |
| **TOEFL Practice Online (TPO)** from ETS | the only full practice test that behaves like the real one and gives official scores per Writing and Speaking task. Save it for once, about two weeks before the exam |

## Careful with

- **Books and courses published before 2026**, and anything that doesn't mention Complete the Words,
  Build a Sentence, Write an Email or Listen and Repeat: that's the old format.
- **Commercial question banks.** Good for volume, not for measuring your level.
- **YouTube** is useful for listening and shadowing input (everyday accents and vocabulary), not
  for measuring.
