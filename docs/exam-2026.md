# The TOEFL iBT, 2026 format, in one page

🔴 **The test changed on January 21, 2026.** If a source talks about Integrated Writing,
Independent Writing, or 0–30 per section as the main score, it describes the old test.

> Summary of the official ETS materials as of late 2026. **When in doubt, the official Test
> Overview PDF and the Official Guide win.** If you find an error here, fix it and leave the
> correction written.

---

## Structure

| Section | Items | Time (approx.) | Tasks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | 50 | ~29 min | Complete the Words · Read in Daily Life · Read an Academic Passage |
| **Listening** | 47 | ~27 min | Listen and Choose a Response · Conversation · Announcement · Academic Talk |
| **Writing** | 12 | ~23 min | Build a Sentence (10) · Write an Email (1, 7 min) · Academic Discussion (1, 10 min) |
| **Speaking** | 11 | ~8 min | Listen and Repeat (7) · Take an Interview (4) |

- **Reading and Listening are multistage adaptive.** Module 1 is the same for everyone (35 Reading
  and 32 Listening questions) and decides whether Module 2 is easier or harder. **Writing doesn't
  adapt.** Some Module 1 items are unscored test items.
- **Listening: no going back.** Each audio plays once, and the clock includes the audio.
- **Speaking: no preparation time.**
- Paper practice tests are **linear**, not adaptive.

## Scoring

| Level | Scale | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Question / task | **0–5** | what a rater gives one email, one Listen and Repeat sentence, one interview answer |
| Section | **1–6**, in half bands | the band on the score report |
| Overall | **1–6** | the **average of the four sections**, rounded to the nearest half band (5.25 → 5.5) |

| CEFR | Band |
|---|---|
| C2 | 6 |
| **C1** | **5 – 5.5** |
| B2 | 4 – 4.5 |
| B1 | 3 – 3.5 |

**A task score of 5 is not C1.** C1 is a section band of 5.0. Don't average task scores and call
it a band.

### Raw points per section (Test Overview, Table 1)

| Section | Raw range | How |
|---|---|---|
| Reading | 0–30 | 1 point per scored question |
| Listening | 0–30 | 1 point per scored question |
| Writing | 0–20 | Build a Sentence 10 × 1 (no partial credit) + Email 0–5 + Discussion 0–5 |
| Speaking | 0–55 | *inferred:* 7 Listen and Repeat × 5 + 4 Interview × 5. Consistent with "each question is scored 0–5", not confirmed by ETS |

**ETS doesn't publish the raw → band conversion** for the real test. The Official Guide has tables
only for its own practice sections.

## Writing details

- **Write an Email:** the instruction is to write as much as you can in complete sentences, with
  **no minimum or maximum**. Purposes in the official material: recommend, invite, propose a solution,
  report a problem, give feedback politely, ask for recommendations, thank and request.
- **Academic Discussion:** *"An effective response will contain at least 100 words."* A professor's
  question plus two classmates' posts (with names). The rubric rewards a clear position, relevant
  elaboration and a concrete example.
- **Build a Sentence:** order words into a sentence; all or nothing.

## Speaking details

- **Listen and Repeat:** 7 sentences on one topic, increasing in length, roughly 8–12 seconds to
  answer each. The rubric tolerates one or two function words missing at the top score; **stopping
  is what drops you** (a "full sentence" separates 3 from 2).
- **Take an Interview:** 4 questions from an interviewer, about 45 seconds per answer, no prep.
- At the test center you get a **headset with microphone**.
